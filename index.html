About David

A promise, kept.

"I made you a promise four years ago: if elected, I would lead with honesty, transparency, and a commitment to listening to our community."

I also pledged to protect our outstanding schools, preserve our safe and welcoming neighborhoods, and safeguard the open spaces that make Loomis special.

As your Council member, and previously as your Mayor, I've worked every day to keep those promises. Throughout my term, I've held monthly round table meetings with residents — something no other Council member has done — because I believe good government starts with listening.

I've been a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars while working with town staff to improve traffic safety and flow, invest in better roads, and strengthen our community through my service as the Council liaison to our local schools and the Loomis Library.

Today, I approach every decision with the same principles I promised from day one: reason, compassion, humility, and common sense. I would be honored to earn your vote and continue serving our community.

— David Ring
